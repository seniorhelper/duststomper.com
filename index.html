Where the name came from

Even the smallest creatures move earth

It started with a guinea pig. Zach watched his daughter’s little one burrowing in its bedding and it hit him: God gave even the smallest creatures the ability to move earth.

That is the heart of Dust Stomper. Every one of us can move a little earth. A kind word, a meal, a prayer, a hand up. Stomp the dust, do good everywhere you can, and give God the glory.

“But ask the animals, and they will teach you, or the birds in the sky, and they will tell you.”
