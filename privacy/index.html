Privacy

Dust Stomper is a small ministry in prayer, run by Zach Wennstedt in Brighton, Colorado. This page explains what happens to what you send us.

What we collect

Only what you type into the contact form: your name, email, optional phone, the topics you check, and your message. We do not use advertising trackers or analytics cookies on this site.

How it is delivered

The form is delivered to our inbox by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), an email forwarding service. Your message passes through their servers on its way to us. The site itself is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

How we use it

We read every message, pray over it, and reply if you asked us to. Prayer requests stay private. We never sell, rent or share your information, and we never post a prayer request publicly without your clear permission.

Your choices

Want your message deleted, or want us to stop contacting you? Just say so through the contact form and we will take care of it.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.
